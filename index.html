George

My work sits at the intersection of molecular biology and computational analysis, using multi-omics to investigate RNA biology and the control of gene expression. To date, my work has focussed on the specific interfaces that drive this regulation during host−pathogen interactions. Using my core wet-lab cell and molecular biology skill-set, I generate large and complex datasets and then build the computational pipelines to interpret them. I bring these findings back to the bench to verify and characterise them. I am passionate about mentoring future scientists and enjoy supervising students for both wet and dry lab projects.

  • RNA biology and the control of gene expression
  • Translation and ribosome dynamics
  • Cell intrinsic immunity and host-pathogen interactions
